Safety and the rules

AI that follows the rules, so you don’t have to think about them.

Calls, texts and AI voices come with real rules. We set your system up with them in mind from day one.

Upfront about being AI

Your assistant can say it’s an AI and that calls may be recorded. California requires everyone’s consent to record a call, so that line is built in.

Recording consent

Texting done right

Business texting needs carrier registration (A2P 10DLC) or messages get blocked. We set it up, and every text honors STOP to opt out.

Registered texting

No cold robocalls

The FCC treats AI voices as “artificial” calls that need consent. So our AI answers and follows up with people who reached out, and never blasts cold calls.

Consent first

Your data, your control

Your customer info is used to run your system, nothing else. We don’t sell it. You approve the build, and a real person is always one step away.

You approve it
